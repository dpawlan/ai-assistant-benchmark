# Persona

Site: https://yourpersona.com/

Persona is a personal AI assistant that works over iMessage and a web dashboard, making calls, booking appointments, handling returns, forms, invoices and subscription cancellations, and coming back with confirmations. It is also pre-selling Persona Band, a wearable wristband front-end for the same assistant that ships December 2026. Requested via the form (issue #310).
