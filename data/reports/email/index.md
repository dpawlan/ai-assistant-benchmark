Muse drafts the reply we would actually send: it declines the proposed day, offers two real slots from the calendar, keeps the tone, and sends on command. Instinct is a point behind and better at tracking a thread over hours. Town is the pick if the inbox is the main thing you want an assistant for.

## Who this is for

For anyone who wants scheduling replies handled from a text without opening the inbox.

## How we tested

The email task is a scheduling reply: decline the proposed day, offer two slots from the calendar, keep the usual tone, and send only when told to.

## The competition

### Instinct

Instinct drafted a correct reply with two real slots and sent on command. It kept tracking the thread and flagged the counter-proposal three hours later without being asked.

### Town

Town read the thread and the calendar directly and drafted a usable reply, though it needed a nudge on tone.
