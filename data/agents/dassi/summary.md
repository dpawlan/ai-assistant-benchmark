# dassi

Site: https://dassi.ai/

dassi is a Chrome/Edge/Brave extension from Omnify Labs that you teach a repeated job once — by describing it or walking it through — after which it reruns that job on a schedule inside the web apps you are already signed into, pausing for the approvals you set (approvable in the side panel or over Telegram). It is bring-your-own-model across Claude, GPT, Gemini and OpenRouter, or dassi's own credits. Requested via the form (issue #303).
