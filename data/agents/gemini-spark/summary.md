# Gemini Spark

Site: https://gemini.google/overview/agent/spark/

Google's 24/7 agent mode inside the Gemini app: tasks, skills and schedules against Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs and Sheets, checking in before major actions. Announced at I/O 2026, on AI Ultra and rolling out to AI Pro in the US since July 2026. Web and app only, no SMS, WhatsApp or iMessage; needs a paid Google AI subscription and an 18+ account in select countries. Requested via the form (issue #221).
