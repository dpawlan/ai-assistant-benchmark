# Norton Family Assistant

Site: https://us.norton.com/products/family-assistant

Norton Family Assistant is Gen Digital's iOS app for parents: it syncs family calendars, summarizes school and activity emails, surfaces the few things that need you today (a moved pickup, a permission slip, a league fee) and lets you clear or snooze them, with the data protections Norton is known for. Free to start; 4.8 on the App Store. Requested via the form (issue #436).
