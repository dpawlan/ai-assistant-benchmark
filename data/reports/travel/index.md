Instinct is the assistant we would let book a flight. It works inside your own airline accounts, picks the aisle without being told, stays under the budget, and checks you in when the window opens. Miso matches it on the booking itself and is worth a look if you already live in its app.

## Who this is for

For people who fly a few times a year and want the booking done from a text, including the boring parts: seat, check-in, boarding pass.

## How we tested

The travel task is a round trip New York to Chicago, out Friday morning and back Sunday evening, aisle seat, under $400, then check-in and the boarding pass when the window opens. We score the booking, the seat, the budget, and whether the follow-ups happen without a reminder.

## Our pick: Instinct

Instinct booked the United round trip inside the tester's own account, chose aisle seats both ways, and came in under budget with a fare it explained in one line. Two days later it sent the boarding pass eight minutes after check-in opened, with no reminder.

### Flaws but not dealbreakers

- It needs your airline login connected before the first booking, which takes a few minutes in its app.

## The competition

### Miso

Miso booked the flight, the dinner and the hotel from a single pasted thread and did not miss a detail. Its conversation lives in its own app, so we could not time it and have not tested check-in.

### Shuffle

Shuffle staged a United round trip with aisle seats both ways under budget and stopped at the payment screen, which is correct behaviour without a card on file.

### Tomo

Tomo found the same fares as Instinct and staged the booking, but asked for the seat preference rather than inferring it.

### Soar

Soar is a travel-only assistant and it shows: fast, precise fares, sensible seat choice. It stops short of check-in.

## What to look forward to

We are adding a change-of-plans task, since rebooking is where most people actually need help.
