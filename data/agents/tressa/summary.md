# Tressa

Site: https://tressa.chat/

Tressa is an agent you text over iMessage, paired with a Mac desktop app that lets it read files and run commands on your machines while the app is open. It keeps routines, connects accounts through Composio, stores credentials in a vault it can use but not read, and writes its own skills when it works out a repeatable job. Requested via the form (issue #374) by the founder, who asks that the Mac app be open during testing.
