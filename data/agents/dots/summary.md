# Dots

Site: https://openai.com/index/introducing-dots/

OpenAI's persistent agentic assistants, announced at Dev Day on 2026-10-01 and powered by GPT-6 Astra. Each Dot runs in its own cloud environment with browser access and keeps working toward a stated goal across connected apps, with Slack and Teams at launch and text messaging said to be coming. Available to ChatGPT Pro and Business Premium subscribers in eligible markets. Requested via the form (issue #465).
