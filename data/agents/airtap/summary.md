# Airtap

Site: https://airtap.ai/

Airtap is an assistant you text over iMessage, SMS or Telegram that runs tasks on a cloud phone: job applications, flight tracking, digital couponing, bookings. It watches your apps, escalates until the goal lands, chains workflows into routines, and streams the phone screen live to a web dashboard so you can watch it tap and type. No app to install.
