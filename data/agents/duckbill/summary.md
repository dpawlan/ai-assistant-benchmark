# Duckbill

Site: https://getduckbill.com/

Duckbill is an execution layer for agents: REST and MCP endpoints for phone calls, payments, errands and services, with a person completing the parts AI cannot finish reliably and the outcome returned as data. It claims 30 million real-world actions and offers its own agent for people without one. Requested via the form (issue #423).
