# ka

Site: https://textka.com/

ka is a personal assistant you text on iMessage (green-bubble SMS fallback on Android) that runs your Gmail, Google Calendar, Docs, Sheets and Drive, reads photos you send, and sends reminders before you ask. It also pitches assistant-to-assistant coordination, where your ka negotiates plans with your friends' ka's. Requested via the form (issue #222).
