# Odessia

Site: https://odessia.com/

AI travel concierge that plans and books flights, hotels (including with rewards points), activities and full itineraries in one web conversation. Launched June 2026 in preview by Francis Davidson, co-founder of Sonder, with $6M led by Sequoia. Free to use, paid by booking commission. Web chat only; no messaging channel or app found.
