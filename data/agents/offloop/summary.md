# Offloop

Site: https://offloop.org/

Offloop is a multiplayer workspace from Intelligence Software, Inc. where small teams run recurring business work — influencer sourcing and outreach, customer follow-up, research, launches, reporting — through named AI agents in persistent Channels with shared context, human approvals and OAuth-scoped connectors. It runs in a web workspace plus macOS and Windows desktop builds, with an iOS TestFlight beta. Requested via the form (issues #227, #306).
