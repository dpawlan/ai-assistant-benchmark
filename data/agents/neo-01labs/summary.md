# Neo

Site: https://01labs.ai/

Neo is Zero One Laboratories' (01labs) personal assistant that runs inside Telegram, with a web account page at 01labs.ai/me and iMessage marked "Coming soon"; it handles text, voice notes, photos and files, connects email and calendar, keeps memory, and sends daily briefings and follow-ups. The site stresses a per-user sealed store that is never sold, shared or trained on. Requested via the form (issue #224).
