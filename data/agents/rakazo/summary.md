# Rakazo

Site: https://rakazo.com/

Open-source bots that sign into your tools and do role work (outbound sales, inbox, recruiting, expenses, bug triage) on Markdown routines, asking for approval with an audit log. Pitched as an open-source Grok Bot alternative; self-hosted today, so on the board as a stretch product. Requested via the form (issue #168).
