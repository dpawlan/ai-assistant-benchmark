# Orlo

Site: https://orlo.today/

Orlo is an invite-only food-ordering assistant: send it a Google Maps link or a craving and roughly where you are, it builds the cart on the restaurant's own site, shows a screenshot of the real checkout with its exact total, and after you approve on your phone pays with a one-time Stripe Link virtual card so your real card never reaches the restaurant. Scope is ordering food and drinks only, so most dimensions are not applicable. Requested via the form (issue #372).
