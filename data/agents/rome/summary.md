# Rome

Site: https://romeos.cc/

Rome (Rome OS) is an MIT-licensed agentic operating system where an agent builds small apps and reusable tools from a prompt, saves actions, and carries context between tasks, with an in-product App Store for those apps. It runs free on your own Mac or self-hosted from github.com/rome-os/rome, or on Rome's hosted always-on machines, and is currently gated behind a private preview invite code.
