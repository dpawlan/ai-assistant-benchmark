For the first few months this site was a scoreboard. Sixteen tests, a number per assistant per test, a head-to-head for any pair. That is the right foundation and it is not going anywhere. But a scoreboard does not answer the question people actually arrive with, which is "which one should I use for this".

Reports do. Each one takes a single question, ranks everyone who has taken the relevant test, names a pick, and explains the pick in enough words that you could disagree with it. They are kept current: when an assistant ships something that changes the answer, we re-run the test and write a dated update that lives in the report and on its own page.

The first one is [The best AI assistant for shopping](/reports/shopping). Travel and email follow this week.

Three rules we are holding ourselves to. Every claim in a report traces to a logged run of the published test. Updates say what we re-ran, not what a press release said. And nothing in a report is sponsored, ever; if we take money for anything it will be an article, labelled as such, and never a pick.
