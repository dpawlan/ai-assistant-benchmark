# Bantr

Site: https://bantr.socialcompanyofus.com/

Bantr is a text-message assistant from The Social Company of the United States that keeps a couple's or family's schedule, budget and investments in one thread, with optional Google Calendar and Gmail connections and a private thread per partner alongside a shared one. It runs entirely over iMessage, SMS and WhatsApp with no app to download. Requested via the form (issue #308).
