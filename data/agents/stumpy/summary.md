# Stumpy

Site: https://stumpy.ai/

Persistent personal agents you text over Telegram, SMS, email or Slack, or chat with on the web: calendar, email triage, reminders, routines and scheduling, with webhooks and MCP for extension. Built by a solo founder, Preston Guillory. Freemium: free tier with one agent and a daily message cap, Personal $50/mo, Pro $200/mo, Enterprise custom. No iMessage or WhatsApp. Requested via the form (issue #343).
